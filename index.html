    The head chef behind ANKRITA

    Hi, I'm Anu.

    Head chef and founder, baking in my little home kitchen.

    The ANKRITA way

    A home kitchen.
    A big heart.

    I've always believed that the best treats are the ones made at home. Simple, honest, and baked for people you care about.

    ANKRITA began in my own kitchen, baking cakes and muffins for family and friends. Word spread, and today I bake for a lot more people, but still the same way: by hand, in small batches, and always fresh.

    I choose wholesome ingredients and keep things balanced, so you can enjoy something delicious and feel good about it too.
